# Swamp Muppet — approved direction, 2026-10-15

Owner: Eric. Primary user: his wife. Axolotl: Rex, dark colored and young.

- App name: **Swamp Muppet**. Brand: **Phoenix Aquatic Systems**.
- Approved reference: in-chat interactive prototype with a dark Rex mascot, deep aquatic surfaces, teal actions, and a hot Phoenix entrance to Eric Mode.
- Only the new tank is managed. The current small tank is out of scope.
- Main path: separate camera and gallery actions → review all readings → save → one next step.
- No generic warning clutter. Explain concrete uncertainty and recovery steps.
- Before move-in: meaningful stages and dated evidence. No fabricated percentage or default zeroes.
- After move-in: the same tank, water checks, care notes, move-in memory, and photo timeline.
- Approximate history is motivational context, never clearance evidence.
- Keep data local, preserve it during upgrades, and keep the installed icon/site identity.
- Updates check on opening online and wait for unfinished work. No promise of updates while closed/offline.
- Technical controls, backup, provenance, experimental analysis, and calculator live in Eric Mode.
- Exact care rules and numeric photo-reader capability were deliberately not frozen before validation.

The implementation does not claim the mockup's illustrated sample is a real reader. Numeric suggestions remain disabled until labeled-photo testing supports enabling them.
